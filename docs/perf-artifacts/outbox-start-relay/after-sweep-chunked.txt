Chunking trades some of the single-flush shape's round-trip reduction for a
bound on claim-hold latency (see docs/performance-outbox-start-relay.md,
"Fix", correction 3). Both floor criteria from the single-flush measurement
still clear at every swept size:
  - "Elimination of an N+1": mark-step statement count is O(n / 8), not
    O(n) -- 7 instead of 50 at n=50, not the single-flush shape's O(claim
    rounds), but still far below one call per row.
  - "≥20% reduction in total buffers for a statement that is ≥5% of the
    workload's buffers": the mark statement is still 58.3% of calls in the
    chunked shape and drops 28.2% on its own buffer count (568 -> 408 at
    n=50), clearing the 20% floor.
